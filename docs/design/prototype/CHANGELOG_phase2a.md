# Phase 2A changelog — phase 1 follow-ups, P1-01 to P1-30, P1-57, P1-58

Files changed: `Personal Finance App.dc.html`. I also corrected three sentences in `App Guide.dc.html` (P1-08, P1-18, P1-30). I did not edit the uploaded .md files.

**How I checked.** I ran the prototype and drove the app's own methods and real button clicks, then read the app state and the rendered DOM. I used the default dataset ("Seven months", today = 16 Aug 2026) unless a row says otherwise. Other datasets were loaded directly into the state.

- **Sizes.** I measured the app area itself: 360 × 576 (a 360 × 640 dp phone after its status and navigation bars), 320 × 504 (320 × 568 dp), 412 × 828, 396 × 812 (the default frame) and 640 × 296 (landscape).
- **Text at 200% was simulated.** I scaled every font size and line height by script. This is not Android sp scaling.
- **Contrast** was measured in the browser against the resolved colour tokens, for all 8 palettes in light and dark (16 pairs).
- **Device tweak.** It now offers "360 × 640 dp (minimum)" and "320 × 568 dp (narrow, must not break)".
